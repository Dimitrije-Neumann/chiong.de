# Chiong

## A social platform combining dating and friendship

Chiong is a web project built around the idea of bringing **dating, friendship and social interaction** together in one platform.

Instead of focusing exclusively on romantic relationships, Chiong is designed to give users different ways to connect with other people depending on what they are looking for.

The project is currently under development.

---

## 🌐 Project

**Website:** https://chiong.de

Chiong is an independently developed web project and part of my ongoing journey into web development and IT.

The goal is to gradually turn the initial concept into a functional and modern social platform.

---

## 💡 Concept

The basic idea behind Chiong is simple:

> People should be able to connect with others without having to define the relationship they are looking for from the beginning.

Depending on their interests and intentions, users can potentially use the platform for:

- Dating
- Friendship
- Meeting new people
- Social interaction
- Discovering people with similar interests
- Communication and messaging

The concept combines elements that are commonly found across dating and social platforms while developing its own structure and user experience.

---

## 🚀 Main Features

The current project concept includes several areas of functionality.

### 👤 User Profiles

Users can create profiles containing information about themselves.

The profile concept is intended to give other users a better understanding of the person behind the account.

Possible profile information includes:

- Profile information
- Interests
- Personal preferences
- Location
- Photos
- Social information

---

### 💬 Messaging

Chiong includes a messaging concept that allows users to communicate with each other.

The goal is to make communication an important part of the platform rather than limiting interaction to profile discovery.

---

### ❤️ Dating

One part of Chiong focuses on dating.

Users can discover other people and potentially find connections based on their interests, preferences and location.

---

### 🤝 Friendship

Chiong is not intended to be exclusively a dating platform.

The friendship aspect allows users to meet people without necessarily looking for a romantic relationship.

This distinction is an important part of the project's concept.

---

### 🔎 Search & Discovery

The project includes the idea of helping users discover other people.

Users should be able to find people based on relevant characteristics and preferences.

The long-term goal is to make discovering compatible people simple and intuitive.

---

### 📍 Locations

Location is another part of the project concept.

The idea is to help users discover people in relevant geographical areas and make local connections easier.

---

### 🎮 Social & Community Elements

Chiong also explores additional social features beyond traditional dating.

The project contains ideas for community-oriented functionality and interactive elements that could make the platform more engaging.

---

## 🧠 Project Vision

The long-term vision for Chiong is to create a platform where **dating and friendship can exist together**.

Many platforms focus heavily on one specific type of relationship.

Chiong explores a different approach:

**One platform – different types of connections.**

The intention is to give users more freedom to decide what kind of connection they want to build.
